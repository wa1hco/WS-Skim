cty.dat: Contest Country Files #3637 (CTY-3637), released 14 November 2026,
by Jim Reisert AD1C, https://www.country-files.com/ (downloaded from
https://www.country-files.com/cty/download/3637/cty-3637.zip).
WS Skim uses it, unmodified, to check that a decoded callsign's prefix is
allocated and that a decoded grid is plausible for the callsign's country.
